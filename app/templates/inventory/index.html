Quick start
  1. Add your products with Add Product, or Import CSV to load many at once.
  2. Get costs in. Click Source missing prices to look them up automatically, or enter them yourself with Bulk Costs, Import CSV, or Paste Sam's.
  3. Optional: click Fill UPCs to add barcodes, which unlock the market-price reference.
  4. Stock the machines. Watch the margin badges and build a shopping list with Restock Run.
Getting costs in (this is what drives your margins)
  1. Source missing prices (the blue button): looks up a wholesale price for every SKU that has none, in the background, and saves the cheapest it finds. One click, no typing. It is a best-effort web lookup, so some SKUs come back empty for you to enter by hand.
  2. Bulk Costs: an inline grid. Type a case price and pack for each SKU, then Save all. The fastest manual way to clear "missing cost".
  3. Import CSV: upload a sheet with columns sku, case_price, case_pack_qty (optional upc). Rows match by SKU then name. Download the template on that page.
  4. Paste Sam's: capture your actual paid prices from your own Sam's order history. Drag the bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar, click it on samsclub.com/orders while signed in, then paste here to review and save. No password sharing.

All of these save onto the product as a supplier cost that feeds Best Source, Cost, and Margin.

UPCs and market pricing (reference only, never a cost)
  1. Fill UPCs: auto-looks-up barcodes for products that don't have one, from the name. Only fills blanks; fix any wrong match on the product's edit form. Capped per run for the daily lookup limit.
  2. You can also set a UPC by hand (Add/Edit form), or load them with the upc column in Import CSV.
  3. Once a product has a UPC, the Market reference panel on Find Product Prices shows what other sellers charge, as a sanity check for your sell price only. It never changes your cost or margin.
Reading the catalog
  • Yellow rows are below par level. Restock with the button.
  • Best Source shows the cheapest supplier on file; Cost and Margin follow that price.
  • Margin badges: green at or above 45%, yellow 30 to 44%, red below 30%.
  • Filter by category or supplier; toggle Low Stock or Seasonal.

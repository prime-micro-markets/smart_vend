{# Find Product Prices tab — embedded in inventory/index.html #}
Find Product Prices is a best-effort web lookup for one product at a time. For reliable costs, use Source missing prices on the Catalog tab (it runs this for every SKU at once) or enter costs manually.
  1. Type a product (for example "Doritos" or "Red Bull 8oz"). It is pre-filled when you came from a specific SKU.
  2. Keep the default vendors checked, unless you want to narrow the search.
  3. Click Find Product Prices. Results show unit and case prices when found; Best marks the lowest unit price.
  • Source badges: Live direct scrape, AI Est. web-search estimate, weak approximate. Verify estimates before ordering.
  • Sam's Club blocks server-side pulls, so it is not listed here. Capture your actual paid prices with Paste Sam's on the Catalog tab instead.
  • Market reference (below results) shows what other sellers charge, for reference only. It never feeds your cost or margin.
  • Lookups run on the live site. On a local machine, outbound calls are usually blocked, so prices only come back on primemicromarkets.com. Use Export CSV on the results page to save a comparison.
